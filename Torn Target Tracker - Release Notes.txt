GETTING STARTED
- Paste a Torn "Limited Access" API key (Torn > Settings > API Keys).
  The page explains everything the key is used for. Torn keys are
  read-only, so the page can't attack, spend, trade or send anything.
- A "Your key never leaves your computer" box explains that the key is
  saved only in this browser, is sent only to api.torn.com, the code is
  open on GitHub, and how to delete it. Tip: make a new key just for
  this page, named something like "Target Tracker".
- Start a list five ways: the Matchup Sheet (Google Sheet link) from Aqua
  leadership, a faction page link, player IDs separated by commas, a
  JSON or CSV file, or one of your saved lists.
- "+ Add targets" adds players without clearing your current list.

MY HOSPITAL ALERTS
- A soft sound plays once when your own hospital time reaches 5:00 and
  a louder one once at 2:00. Each plays once per hospital stay and
  resets when you leave or start a new stay. Uses the same status check
  as the top bar, so no extra API calls.
- Both alert times can be changed (First alert / Final alert, in
  minutes:seconds from 0:10 to 60:00; defaults 5:00 and 2:00). Changing
  a time re-arms that alert.
- Settings > My hospital alerts: turn each alert on or off, set its
  volume, and pick a sound (Soft chime, Double beep, Alarm, Bell).
  "Test" plays it.
- "Add your own sound" lets you add an audio file (up to about 1 MB),
  kept in this browser. Pick it for either alert, or delete it.
- Browsers block sound until you click the page once; a small hint
  shows in Settings until sound is allowed.

TARGET LIST
- Sorting: click a column header to sort by it (click again to flip).
  Time sorts by time left (Okay counts as 0, no timer goes last).
- "Pin Status" (on by default) keeps Status groups on top (Okay,
  Hospital, Traveling, Jail, Abroad, then the rest) and sorts by the
  clicked column inside each group. Turn it off to sort the whole list.
- Click Status to flip the group order: ▲ Okay first (then Hospital,
  Traveling, ...), ▼ reversed (Okay last). The direction is kept while
  sorting by other columns.
  The sort and the pin are remembered.
- Spreadsheet-style grid that fits on one screen without side scrolling.
- Columns: Name [ID] (links to profile), Level, Life bar (hover for
  numbers), Status, Time, Reason, History (wins/losses vs. each player),
  Active, Group, Priority star (bolds the name), Stats, Attack button,
  editable Notes, and a trash can to remove a player.
- Hospital/jail/travel countdowns synced with Torn's clock. At 0 the timer
  turns green and counts up "Out for ..." until the player is re-checked.
- Click any column header to sort; click again to reverse. Status sorts
  Hospital (soonest out first), then OK, then everyone else.
- Rows stay in place while the list updates; new players go to the bottom.
- Click a player's row to refresh just that player.
- "Aqua vs <faction>" heading for lists made from a sheet or faction page,
  with links to the target sheet and your faction page.
- The page scrolls until the column headers reach the top, then the list
  scrolls on its own.

DASHBOARD (header, beside the "Aqua vs ..." title)
- Cards for: Energy, Nerve and Life as bars with numbers; your status
  with a big countdown (red card while in hospital); drug, booster and
  medical cooldowns with their max (adjustable in Settings); ranked war
  (your score vs theirs, lead or deficit vs the target with a bar,
  running time, War page link, or "No ranked war"); chain count vs the
  next bonus as a bar with the chain timer (amber under 60s, red under
  30s); and the Torn City Time (TCT) clock.
- STAY IN HOSP alert from messages saying "Turtle", "Stay in Hosp" or
  "Stay in Hospital". It turns into a green PUSH when the push time hits.
- Push countdown read from message subjects with "push" and a TC time
  (e.g. "Push @ 18:30", "17:40 Push"), shown as "Push @ 18:30 in 1h 12m".
- Push timer alerts: a sound at 5:00 and at 2:00 before the push
  (both times adjustable, like the hospital alerts) (once
  per push time; a new push time re-arms them). Settings > Push timer
  alerts: on/off, volume and sound for each (defaults Double beep 50%
  and Alarm 90%), with Test, and your own sounds work here too. The
  5-minute pop-up message still follows the pop-up setting.
- Faction call alerts: a sound when the call changes to STAY IN HOSP
  and a different one when it changes to PUSH (once per new message or
  change; a call already showing when the page opens stays quiet).
  Settings > Faction call alerts: on/off, sound, volume and Test for
  each (defaults Bell 70% and Alarm 90%); your own sounds work too.
- Rearranges for smaller screens and works in light and dark themes.
- Slim sticky mini-bar slides in at the top after you scroll past the
  dashboard, so your hospital, chain timer and TCT stay in view.

UPDATES AND API USE
- Refresh All checks every player (about 650 ms apart, ~92 calls a
  minute, under Torn's 100-a-minute limit). Other lookups pause until
  it's done.
- Optional automatic updates; the top bar keeps updating during them.
- Messages checked every 5 seconds, your status every 5 seconds, war
  score once a minute, battle history every 5 minutes.
- Reloading the page (F5) does not re-check the whole list.
- After 15 minutes with no mouse, keyboard or scroll activity, all Torn
  calls stop until you're back.
- An API key not used for 7 days is deleted from the browser.

SETTINGS (slide-in drawer on the right)
- Change, show or delete your API key right in the drawer.
- Automatic updates and Target List Refresh Speed.
- Battle history size, clock fine-tuning (countdowns and TCT clock).
- When you're away: pause time and API key expiry.
- Cooldown limits (medical / booster max).
- Backup or Restore lists: download JSON or CSV, save this list, saved
  lists (restore, rename, download, delete), copy player IDs.
- New sheet / Reset list (your current list is saved first).
- Erase everything from this browser: deletes your key, lists, notes,
  history and settings so the page starts like new.
- Dark / light mode.
